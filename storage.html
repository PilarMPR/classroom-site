Cookies y almacenamiento

Última actualización: 25 de octubre de 2026

Read in English

✓En resumen
  • El sitio no usa cookies.
  • Guarda en tu navegador solo lo que hace falta para las funciones que tú usas: ajustes y progreso.

  • Por eso no hay banner de cookies: la ley no lo exige para este tipo de almacenamiento.

Todo es almacenamiento local de tu navegador (localStorage, sessionStorage e IndexedDB), propio de rojoclassroom.app. Ningún otro sitio puede leerlo.

Por qué no pedimos consentimiento

El artículo 22.2 de la LSSI-CE (y el art. 5.3 de la Directiva ePrivacy) exige consentimiento para guardar datos en tu dispositivo, salvo cuando son estrictamente necesarios para un servicio que tú pides. La guía de cookies de la AEPD incluye aquí las preferencias que eliges (como el tamaño de letra) y los datos que tú introduces (como tus marcas). Es exactamente lo que hace este sitio, y nada más.

Cómo borrarlo

  • Chrome / Edge: pulsa el icono a la izquierda de la dirección → Configuración del sitio → Borrar datos.
  • Firefox: icono del candado → Borrar cookies y datos del sitio.
  • Safari: Ajustes → Privacidad → Gestionar datos de sitios web → rojoclassroom.app → Eliminar.

En un ordenador compartido del colegio, usa una ventana privada: todo se borra al cerrarla.

Si esto cambia

Si algún día añadimos analítica, vídeos incrustados u otra cosa que no sea estrictamente necesaria, te pediremos permiso antes y actualizaremos esta página.

Legal and privacy

Cookies and storage

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Leer en español

✓In short
  • The site uses no cookies.
  • It saves in your browser only what the features you use need: settings and progress.
  • None of it is sent anywhere. No analytics, no advertising, no third parties.
  • That is why there is no cookie banner: the law does not require one for this kind of storage.

What is saved

NameWhat it is forHow long
cfzlib.*Reading settings (font, size, spacing, theme, voice), current step, ticked lessons and steps, quiz scores and flashcardsUntil you delete it
igcse.*Progress and settings in the IGCSE library (Years 10–11)Until you delete it
t3.* · bml.* · ciw.* · sr.*Progress inside some practice tools (binary, bit maths lab, code in words, circuits)Until you delete it
demo.session (session)Pretend log-in in the “How the web works” demo. It is fake: it only shows how a cookie works.Until you close the tab
cq-arcade-v1Computing Quest Arcade: the name you type (if you do), your answers and your progressUntil you delete it
tk-maker-course-v1 · tk-maker-ex-v1 · tk-maker-bc-v1Tinkercad Maker Course: lessons, exercises and builds doneUntil you delete it
pcBuildBench.v2PC Build Bench: your progress building the PCUntil you delete it
IndexedDB classroom-studentThe keys that open your year’s pages (and the shared ones) after you type the code from the board. They cannot be read or exported; the code itself is never saved.180 days for each year
Service worker (class site)Decrypts the class pages inside your browserUntil you clear the site data
cls-restart (session)Stops a loop while the class site opensUntil you close the tab

All of it is your browser's own storage (localStorage, sessionStorage and IndexedDB) for rojoclassroom.app. No other website can read it.

Why we do not ask for consent

Article 22.2 of Spain's LSSI-CE (and article 5(3) of the EU ePrivacy Directive) needs your consent before saving data on your device, except when it is strictly necessary for a service you ask for. Spain's data protection authority (AEPD) counts preferences you choose (like text size) and data you enter (like your ticks) as exempt. That is exactly what this site does, and nothing else.

How to delete it

  • Chrome / Edge: click the icon left of the address → Site settings → Clear data.
  • Firefox: padlock icon → Clear cookies and site data.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → rojoclassroom.app → Remove.

On a shared school computer, use a private window: everything is deleted when you close it.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics, embedded videos or anything not strictly necessary, we will ask you first and update this page.
